Snuggle Arrows: Cute Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game "Snuggle Arrows: Cute Puzzle" (the "Game"), published on Google Play by Walidev35 ("we", "us"), handles information.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, hints, hearts, avatars, chosen theme, daily reward streak and settings) is stored only on your own device. It is not sent to us. If you uninstall the Game, this data is deleted.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and process:

• Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID

• Approximate location derived from your IP address

• Device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version)

• Ad interaction data (for example, which ads were shown or tapped)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Learn more at:

• How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

• Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

3. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are used. To change your choice later, clear the Game's data (Android Settings › Apps › Snuggle Arrows › Storage › Clear data) and the form will be shown again the next time you open the Game.

4. Rewarded ads

Some optional rewards (extra hints, extra hearts, doubled coins or daily rewards) can be earned by watching a video ad. Watching these ads is always your choice.

5. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads in the Game are limited to content rated for general audiences.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share any information with third parties other than Google AdMob as described above.

7. Security and retention

Game progress stays on your device. Information processed by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's policies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this address, with the date of the last update.
